At my husband's funeral, a little boy at the back of the church smiled at the stained-glass window.

A crooked smile, higher on the left. I had looked at that exact smile across the breakfast table for forty-seven years. 💔

Two weeks later, in Howard's desk, I found 34 years of money orders, every single month, to a woman I had never heard of.
